      How the wheel works

      1. Set your entries. Type names, tasks or options, or pick a template.
      2. Spin the wheel. Click “Spin the wheel”, the centre of the wheel, or press the space bar.
      3. See the winner. The wheel slows down and shows the result. If you like, remove the winner and spin again.

      The wheel runs right in your browser, needs no app and saves your entries automatically on your device.

      Frequently asked questions

      How does the online wheel work?

      Enter your options, click spin and the wheel stops at a random entry. The result is shown immediately.

      Is the wheel really random?

      Yes. The winner is chosen by your browser's random number generator and every entry has the same chance.

      Can I save my own entries?

      Yes. Your entries are saved automatically in your browser and are there again next time.

      Does the wheel cost anything?

      No, it is free and needs neither sign-up nor installation.

      Does it work on mobile?

      Yes, the page adapts automatically to smartphones, tablets and PCs.
